# DR-0003 · Product Core mandate v0.2.0: the organisation, its processes and its paths

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION, by merging this record (the decider is a position)
- **Source:** the design of the first instance of Product Core, decided by that instance's governing seat between 19 and 20 October 2026 and reviewed in five rounds on four engine families; the governing seat's direction of 20 October 2026 (translated): "In the Library, write it generally, not on our terms."
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0001 and DR-0002; it supersedes nothing. Where `MANDATE.md` differs from blueprint `v0.1.1`, the mandate governs from this merge.

## Decision

1. **The mandate.** `MANDATE.md` states, for any organisation, Product Core's organisation, the reasons for it, its sixteen processes and its paths with the human hub, the engineering AIOC, the engine division, the marketing division and the other parts. Each organisation's values are data (`MANDATE.md` §8).
2. **The shape.** A CPO Office of twelve agents with Discovery and Incubation inside it; one Design and Brand Studio with two human seats; a COS per category; one product team of twelve agents per entry, each a complete small company for its product.
3. **Handing acts to a COS.** A human seat may hand any of its work or decisions, the human acts included, to its own COS, on its own responsibility, by a delegation it signs. Each act is recorded as the seat's own act, so TEST-PC v1, case 42 holds. What the law, a regulator or the source of record gives to a natural person, and a two-person control, stay with people.
4. **Default times and stand-ins.** Every human act has a default time; every act except R5 and R8 has a stand-in. Nothing approves itself.
5. **R6.** The governing seat sets which versions it approves itself; the mother's default keeps the first version of each new product and every major version with it, and gives every minor version and patch to the CPO, handed to its COS.
6. **Seats.** The CPO opens Category Lead seats when it wants. The manager of any agent position may make it a human seat, and turn it back. The same rule is proposed for every part of an `[ORG]AION` (`upstream/PROPOSALS.md`, item 3).
7. **The next version.** Blueprint `v0.2.0` carries the mandate into its sections, its fixed test (TEST-PC v2) and its cards; the Library pin moves then, by a pull request to `49GAION-protocol`.

## Why

A product division run almost wholly by agents needs three things the first blueprint did not yet state: a team per product that owns its whole outcome, checkers that no maker controls, and human seats that never become the queue. The first instance's design gives all three, and its reviews scored it 8 to 9 of 10.
